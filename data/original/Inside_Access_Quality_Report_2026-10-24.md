# Inside Access — first opportunities database

**Research date: 24 October 2026. Status: private review draft.**

The selected database contains **30 verified event or targeted casting records** across Amsterdam, London and Paris. A separate queue holds **6 pending candidates**, and **12 unsuitable records** are excluded. **22 discovery sources** are recorded separately.

“Verified” means the published event or specific production brief and its access route were checked using organizer, venue, broadcaster or audience-provider evidence. It does **not** mean a seat is reserved, an application will succeed, every guest will attend, or live inventory was checked through checkout. Each row records that distinction.

## Coverage and limits

Selected records cover film festivals and premieres, public conversations, a dated red-carpet fan-pen request, TV audiences, targeted background casting, fashion-related events, pop-ups and special culture.

**No confirmed award-show seat-filler vacancy or in-scope stand-in booking was found.** The input’s specific stand-in call is in Leiden. Audience participation, paid recordings, fan pens and seat filling are different access methods. No official Paris Fashion Week runway admission is claimed.

The three pay/allowance routes are a €60 Amsterdam casting allowance, a £50 London audience fee, and a London production brief advertising industry rates without a published amount. Pay does not imply selection. Work eligibility and agency deductions need checking before accepting a booking.

Deadline fields remain blank where no fixed deadline is published. The adjacent status explains whether no deadline was published or no application is needed. Event dates have not been substituted for deadlines. Unpublished prices remain blank with explanations; blank never means free.

Seven medium-confidence rows expose specific limitations: unretrieved inventory, recurring-date selection, a month-only casting window, a rate-limited RSVP provider, differing general versus event-specific DocLab pricing, or incomplete supporting event metadata. The database does not invent the missing facts.

## Import and publication controls

1. Ingest into a private candidate queue with source URL, observed status, retrieval date and source record ID.
2. Normalize localities, dates, currencies, access and eligibility. Leave unknown values null.
3. Deduplicate by organizer/production, title, actual location, occurrence and access product. Related festival products share a parent ID. Do not create one record per day to inflate counts.
4. Require evidence for the event and public route. Put unclear identity, conflicting material conditions or unresolved dates in pending review.
5. At refresh, exclude passed event windows/deadlines, closed or cancelled calls, and sold-out products. Recheck urgent casting and fan pens before use.
6. Recheck primary conditions before travel or payment. A visible form is not a seat guarantee.
7. Keep approval at Awaiting approval. Publication requires a separate decision.

The selected CSV is an **offline draft feed**. It contains no private applicant names, contact details, photographs, identity documents, submissions or account data.
